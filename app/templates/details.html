Our story
Cooking with heart since 2008
What started as a small family kitchen in Visakhapatnam has grown into one of the city's most beloved bakery and cake shops. We provide customised cakes and pastries, snack boxes and freshly baked products, and serve Chinese, Continental and Italian food as well.
"Every dish we make carries a story — of tradition, family, and the love of good food."
We bake fresh every day with local ingredients. Nothing artificial. Nothing frozen. Just honest, delicious food.
